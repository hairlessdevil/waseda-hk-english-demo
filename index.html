OUR PURPOSE

Philosophy

Connecting Japan and Hong Kong.
Bringing capital, technology, people and knowledge together.
Creating new business value.

Waseda HK Capital connects universities, research institutions, businesses and investors, with a focus on Japan and Hong Kong. We support research and development in advanced technologies, commercialization and international business expansion.

By bringing Japan’s advanced technologies and research achievements together with Hong Kong’s international financial capabilities, investor networks and business infrastructure, we help businesses expand across Asia.
